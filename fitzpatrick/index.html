Fitzpatrick skin assessment
Clinical note
This questionnaire is a screening aid only. Final suitability, laser parameters and patch testing decisions should be confirmed during professional consultation and clinical assessment.

1) What is your natural hair colour? (Before any colouring or dyeing)

2) What is your natural eye colour?

3) What is your natural skin colour on an area that is not usually exposed to the sun?

4) Freckles on parts of the skin not exposed to the sun

5) If you spend a whole day in strong summer sun without sunscreen, what usually happens?

6) After spending several days in the sun, how much colour (tan) does your skin develop?

7) Can your skin develop a noticeable tan after just one sunny day?

8) How does your face usually react to sunlight?

9) When was the last time you had significant sun exposure or used a sunbed?

10) How often is the treatment area exposed to sunlight?
