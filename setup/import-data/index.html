Migration safeguards

What Eselram imports

  • Customers: contact details, notes and marketing consent.
  • Bookings: past/future appointments, status, price, discounts, notes, package links and optional future reminders.
  • Packages/courses: session totals, dates, status, price/discount and linked completed/future sessions.
  • Payments: historical paid/due/refund records linked to a booking or package. Imported records never create Stripe charges.
  • Vouchers: active/inactive amount or percentage voucher definitions for future use.
  • No historical confirmation, receipt or reminder emails are sent while importing.
  • External references are strongly recommended to prevent duplicates and link sheets safely.
  • Structured clinical form submissions/photos are intentionally not imported by this generic workbook because their structure differs between systems.
