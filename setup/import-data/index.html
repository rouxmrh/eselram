Simplified workbook

What you fill in

  • Customers: name, email, phone, notes and marketing consent.
  • Bookings: customer, Eselram service, date/time, status, price, amount already paid, optional package/course and notes.
  • Packages & Courses: customer, Eselram package/course, dates, status, price and amount already paid.
  • Treatment History: optional standard historical treatment details.
  • Vouchers: optional voucher definitions.
  • No separate Payments sheet and no technical customer/booking IDs are required.
  • Completed bookings linked to a package/course are what Eselram uses to count used package sessions.
  • Imported payments are historical/manual only. Eselram never creates Stripe charges or refunds during import.
  • No historical confirmation, payment or reminder emails are sent.
